Self-hosted form backend

Form submissions for static sites.

Point your <form> at your own server. One Go binary, one SQLite file, one Docker Compose command. No per-submission fees, no vendor lock-in, no surprise pricing.

Spam filter

No CAPTCHA. No third party reading your mail.

A honeypot field, a per-IP token bucket, and a deliberately conservative weighted score over link markup, SQL probes, keyword hits and synthetic-looking text. Held submissions go to a quarantine you can review — not a black hole.

Webhooks

Told wherever you already look.

Configured per form in the admin UI, fired in a background goroutine so the visitor's response never waits. Email stays optional. A Test Webhook button verifies your endpoint before you go live.

Waitlist mode

A form notifies you. A waitlist emails them back.

Collect unique signups by email, hand each person their place in line, and broadcast to the whole list when you launch — all self-hosted, no mailing-list SaaS.
